The problem

Modern work is wearing your workforce down

Long hours at desks and on the line take a quiet toll on health, safety and morale, and most wellness programs can't show whether they're helping.

⌛

Sedentary, unhealthy habits

Hours of sitting, skipped movement and poor sleep add up to lower energy and rising health costs.

⚠

Ergonomic strain and injury

Repetitive tasks, poor posture and awkward workstations lead to pain, lost time and claims.

↘

Disengagement

Generic perks get ignored. Without participation and data, it's hard to justify the budget.

The solution

Wellness challenges + a KPI-tracking app, working together

We design and run company-wide wellness challenges that make healthy habits social and fun. The Workforce Wellness app tracks participation and progress, so you can see impact, not just guess at it.

Wellness challenges

  • Movement, steps and active-break challenges
  • Team and department competitions
  • Ergonomics, sleep and stress-management themes
  • Coach-designed, ready to launch

The Workforce Wellness app

  • Employees join, log and track progress
  • Leaderboards and streaks keep momentum
  • Real-time participation and engagement KPIs
  • Reports HR can share with leadership

How it works

From kickoff to measurable results in four steps

  1. 1

    Discover

    We learn your goals, workforce and culture, and agree on the KPIs that define success.

  2. 2

    Design

    We build a challenge calendar tailored to your teams, locations and work styles.

  3. 3

    Launch

    Employees join in the app. We support kickoff, communications and coaching content.

  4. 4

    Measure

    Track participation and progress on your dashboard and refine the program with real data.

About the founder

Salim Heyaime

Founder · Coach · Workplace wellness professional

Salim is a coach dedicated to helping people build strength, balance and confidence in their bodies and their lives. His path into wellness started from a personal desire for better health, longevity and functionality, and grew into a mission to help others discover what they're capable of.

He brings together experience in fitness, nutrition, yoga and CrossFit, and specializes in breathwork, meditation, sleep hygiene and stress management. His approach is about sustainable habits, not quick fixes, and Workforce Wellness brings that same philosophy to entire organizations.

He is also the author of A Challenge to Move, a 22-day roadmap built around seven minutes of movement a day.
